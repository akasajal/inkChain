The Paper Harbor
Final draft

When Mara returned to Bellwether, the harbor clock was still seven minutes slow. Beneath a loose floorboard in the old ticket office, she found her father's brass key, just where he had promised it would be.

It opened a narrow cabinet behind the station map. Inside, a bundle of letters rested under a ferry timetable from the winter the island lighthouse went dark. Blue thread held the pages together.

At the bottom was an envelope addressed to Mara. The handwriting slanted left—the same unmistakable hand that had filled the margins of her childhood storybooks.

She carried the letters to the seawall. The abandoned lighthouse waited at the end of the breakwater, its windows catching the last light.

Mara had come to sell the ticket office. She unfolded the timetable and found the final boat to the island. There was time to catch it, if she ran.
