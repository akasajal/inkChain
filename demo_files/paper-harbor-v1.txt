The Paper Harbor
First draft

When Mara returned to Bellwether, the harbor clock was still seven minutes slow. She found her father's brass key beneath the loose board in the old ticket office, exactly where he had said it would be.

The key opened a narrow cabinet behind the station map. Inside lay a bundle of letters tied with blue thread and a ferry timetable dated the winter the island lighthouse went dark.

At the bottom of the bundle, one envelope carried her name. The handwriting leaned sharply to the left.

Mara took the letters to the seawall. Across the water, the abandoned lighthouse stood at the end of the breakwater, its windows pale in the afternoon haze.

She had come to sell the ticket office. Instead, she unfolded the timetable and began looking for the last boat of the evening.
