The Paper Harbor
Revised draft

When Mara returned to Bellwether, the harbor clock was still seven minutes slow. She found her father's brass key beneath the loose floorboard in the old ticket office, exactly where he had promised it would be.

The key opened a narrow cabinet hidden behind the station map. Inside lay a bundle of letters tied with blue thread and a ferry timetable dated the winter the island lighthouse went dark.

At the bottom of the bundle, one envelope carried her name. The handwriting slanted sharply to the left—the same hand that had filled the margins of her childhood storybooks.

Mara carried the letters to the seawall. Across the water, the abandoned lighthouse stood at the end of the breakwater, its windows bright against the afternoon haze.

She had come to sell the ticket office. Instead, she unfolded the timetable and searched for the last boat to the island.
